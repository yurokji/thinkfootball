## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Docushot turns your phone into a powerful document scanner. Scan receipts, contracts, notes, ID cards, and more — all processed locally on your device for maximum privacy.

📸 SMART DOCUMENT SCANNING
• Point your camera and scan instantly with Google ML Kit
• Auto edge detection for perfectly cropped documents
• Scan multiple pages into a single document

✂️ CROP & ROTATE
• 4-point perspective correction for skewed documents
• Free rotation for perfect alignment
• Re-edit crops anytime with saved corner positions

🎨 FILTERS & ENHANCEMENT
• Magic Color: auto-enhance for clean, readable scans
• Black & White: classic document look
• Lighten: brighten dark scans
• Manual brightness & contrast adjustment

📝 OCR TEXT RECOGNITION
• Extract text from scanned documents
• Supports 5 scripts: Latin, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Devanagari
• Copy extracted text to clipboard instantly

📄 FLEXIBLE EXPORT
• Export as PDF (A4, auto orientation)
• Share individual images
• ZIP export for bulk sharing (Premium)

📁 DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT
• Organize documents with custom titles
• Drag & drop page reordering
• Search by title or OCR text
• Merge multiple documents (Premium)
• Batch operations (Premium)

💾 BACKUP & RESTORE
• Create full backups as ZIP files
• Restore from local backups (Premium)

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• All processing happens on your device
• No cloud uploads, no account required
• No analytics or tracking

⭐ PREMIUM FEATURES
